## 📖 Description

حاسبة الاعدايدية المجانية تساعد الطلاب المصريين على حساب الدرجة المطلوبة في الامتحان النهائي للوصول إلى المجموع المستهدف. 

A free E3dadia grade calculator that helps Egyptian students calculate the score needed on final exams to reach their target overall grade.

This project was created to reduce exam anxiety by replacing uncertainty with clarity.

## 🎯 Features

- ✅ **Egyptian grading system** - Uses 40% (terms) / 60% (final exam) formula
- ✅ **Arabic interface** - Designed specifically for Egyptian students
- ✅ **Real-time calculation** - Instant results with smooth animations
- ✅ **Study tips** - 15 random tips to help students prepare
- ✅ **Mobile responsive** - Works perfectly on phones and tablets
- ✅ **WhatsApp sharing** - Easy sharing with classmates
